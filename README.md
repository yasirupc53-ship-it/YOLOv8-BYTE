# YOLOv8-BYTE

**YOLOv8-BYTE: Ship tracking algorithm using short-time sequence SAR images for disaster response leveraging GeoAI**

Published in *International Journal of Applied Earth Observation and Geoinformation*, Volume 128 (2024), Article 103771.

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2024.103771

## Overview

The study combines an improved YOLOv8 detector with a refined ByteTrack algorithm for ship tracking in short-time sequence synthetic aperture radar (SAR) images.

## Dataset availability

The study dataset is confidential and is not uploaded to this repository. The published paper states that data will be made available on request. Requests should be directed to the corresponding author and are subject to applicable confidentiality restrictions and permissions.
